Why study with others for JEE?

JEE preparation means long hours on your own, and that is where most study plans fall apart. When you can see other aspirants working at the same moment, it becomes easier to start a session and stay in your seat until it ends.

How a CrackRoom study room works

  • Pick a room for JEE, for your class, or for one subject.
  • Follow one shared timer. Everyone in the room focuses and takes breaks together.
  • Stay on task. Save the chat for breaks, then get back to work when the next block starts.
  • Host your own room with your own timings, and keep it private with an invite code if you want to study with friends.

Using rooms for Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics

Different subjects need different kinds of attention. Problem practice in Mathematics and Physics usually benefits from longer unbroken blocks, while revising Chemistry notes can suit shorter ones. You can choose a room that fits, or host one with the timing you want. A planner helps here: decide the night before which chapter each session is for, so the first minutes of a block go to studying and not to choosing what to study.

More than a timer

  • A study planner to organise sessions by subject and day.
  • A personal focus timer with a record of your consistency.
  • A notes library organised by exam, subject and chapter.
  • A community to share wins and struggles, and 1:1 mentorship sessions you can book.
