Questions students ask

Is CrackRoom free?

Yes. You can use the study rooms, timers, planner, notes and community for free, and you only need a Google account to sign in.

What is an online study room?

It is a shared space where students study at the same time. Seeing others focus, and following one timer together, makes it easier to sit down and keep going.

Who is it for?

Students preparing for JEE or NEET, including Class 11, Class 12 and droppers.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. CrackRoom works in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, so there is nothing to install.
